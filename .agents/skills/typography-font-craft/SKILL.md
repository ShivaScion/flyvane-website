# Typography & Font Craft

## 1. Font Selection & Curated Pairings
- **Editorial / Bespoke Portfolio:** `Instrument Serif`, `Fraunces`, or `Newsreader` (Headings) + `Geist Sans`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, or `General Sans` (Body).
- **Engineering / Tech Precision:** `Space Grotesk` or `Cabinet Grotesk` (Headings) + `Commit Mono` or `Geist Mono` (Code/Meta) + `Geist Sans` (Body).

## 2. Optical Spacing & Micro-Typography
- **Large Headings (`text-4xl`–`text-7xl`):** `tracking-[-0.03em]` to `tracking-[-0.04em]`, `leading-[1.05]` to `leading-[1.12]`, `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Small Caps / Labels (`text-xs`–`text-sm`):** `uppercase`, `tracking-[0.08em]` to `tracking-[0.12em]`, `font-medium`, `text-neutral-400`.
- **Body Copy:** `leading-relaxed` (1.6–1.75 line-height), `max-w-prose`, `text-wrap: pretty`.
